We are going to build a very simple light weight well designed desktop app for both windows and mac os. 

The 20-20-20 rule is a simple guideline to help reduce digital eye strain and fatigue from looking at screens.

This will be a small widget which is basically eyes living on the homescreen, navbar or widget bar on mac. 

Well designed eyes, turning red as 20 mins pass, and shows a reminder with 20 seconds to look 20 feet away. 

Lets create a very basic mvp first and then design it very beautifully. We will open source this.
