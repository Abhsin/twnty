Install in a minute

twnty isn't code-signed yet, so your system will ask you to confirm the first launch. It's a one-time step, and the source is public if you'd like to check it first.

macOS

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg file.
  2. Drag twnty into your Applications folder.
  3. Open twnty once. When macOS shows a warning, click Done.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway (macOS 15 and later).

Windows

  1. Run the downloaded installer (.exe).
  2. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway, then follow the installer.

Wrong system? Get the other version.

What to expect

  • A pair of eyes appears in your menu bar (macOS) or system tray (Windows). On Windows, click the taskbar's hidden-icons arrow (^) if you don't see them.
  • Over 20 minutes, they slowly redden and droop.
  • When time's up, a calm 20-second break appears. Look about 20 feet away, and the eyes perk back up.
