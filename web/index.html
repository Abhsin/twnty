Thoughtfully built

Menu bar & system tray

Dynamic high-DPI eye icons with 10 progressive fatigue stages and an optional live countdown on macOS.

Optional floating eyes

Pin transparent SVG eyes anywhere on your desktop—they blink naturally, track your cursor, and smile after breaks.

Card or full-screen break

Choose a centered floating card or a full-screen breathing field, with strict mode if you want no skip button.

Pauses when you step away

Automatic idle and screen-lock detection pauses after 5 minutes away and resets fresh when you return.

Respects full-screen apps

Defers popup windows during videos, games, or presentations and sends a quiet native notification instead.

Native Rust, zero bloat

Background timer runs in Rust with local-only settings and stats. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.
