First launch on macOS or Windows

twnty isn't code-signed yet, so your OS may warn you the first time. It's a one-time step, and the source is public if you'd like to check it first.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag twnty to Applications.
  2. Open twnty once. When macOS warns you, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm.

Windows

  1. Run the installer.
  2. On the SmartScreen screen, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.

The 20-20-20 rule, made hard to ignore

It's a simple habit: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. twnty keeps the clock so you don't have to.

  1. 20 min

    Every 20 minutes

    The eyes sit quietly in your menu bar or tray, reddening and drooping as screen time builds.

  2. 20 feet

    Look 20 feet away

    Let your focus soften onto a distant window, tree, or wall across the room.

  3. 20 sec

    For 20 seconds

    Breathe through the calm countdown until a soft chime plays and the eyes wake up clear.

The American Optometric Association recommends the 20-20-20 rule as a simple habit for digital eye strain. The evidence is encouraging but modest, so think of twnty as a nudge toward better screen habits, not a treatment. Read the 20-20-20 rule guide

Built to stay out of your way

Optional floating eyes

Card or full-screen break

Pick a floating card or a full-screen breathing field. Strict mode hides the Skip button.

Pauses when you step away

Questions, answered

Does the 20-20-20 rule work?

The American Optometric Association recommends it as a simple habit for digital eye strain: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Studies are encouraging but modest, and the exact numbers are not proven thresholds. twnty is a habit tool, not a medical device or a treatment. Read the details in our guide to the 20-20-20 rule.

Is twnty really free?

Yes. twnty is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account, no subscription and no paid tier.

Is it safe? Why does macOS or Windows warn me when I open it?

The current builds are not code-signed yet, so macOS Gatekeeper and Windows SmartScreen show a warning for unrecognized apps. On macOS, open twnty once, click Done on the warning, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. The full source is public on GitHub, so you can read it or build it yourself.

Does twnty track me?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Your settings and break stats stay on your computer.

Does twnty work on Linux?

Not yet. twnty currently runs on macOS (Universal, so Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows.

Will it interrupt fullscreen video or presentations?

No. When a full-screen app, game or presentation is in front, twnty holds back the break popup and sends a quiet notification instead.

Can I make it stricter?

Yes. Turn on strict mode to hide the Skip button, and choose a full-screen break instead of the floating card.
