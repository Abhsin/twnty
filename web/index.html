Questions, answered

Does the 20-20-20 rule work?

The American Optometric Association recommends it for digital eye strain, though clinical evidence is modest. twnty is a habit reminder, not a medical treatment. See our guide.

Is twnty really free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT license, with no account or paid tier.

Is it safe? Why does macOS or Windows warn me when I open it?

Current builds aren't code-signed yet, so macOS and Windows show a one-time warning. On macOS, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway; on Windows, click More info → Run anyway. The full source is public on GitHub.

Does twnty track me?

No. The app has no accounts, analytics, or telemetry, and the website uses only cookieless page-view stats (privacy policy).

Does twnty work on Linux?

Not yet. twnty runs on macOS and Windows.

Will it interrupt full-screen video or presentations?

No. It holds back the popup and sends a quiet notification instead.

Can I make it stricter?

Yes. Turn on strict mode to hide the Skip button, and switch to a full-screen break.
